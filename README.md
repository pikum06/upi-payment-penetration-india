## Abstract
India is a developing country progressing rapidly in terms of technology advancements and infrastructure development. Out of all sectors, the banking and finance sector has shown significant growth after the central bank permitted digital payment modes. In this study, the depth to which the UPI mode of payment has reached the grassroot level in India is analyzed. A thorough comparative analysis among metropolitan states and non-metropolitan states has been conducted using financial data sourced from Kaggle.
